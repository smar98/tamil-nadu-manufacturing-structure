# Related work

This note compares the project with published analyses of the same surveys and with Tamil Nadu policy documents. Each source was checked against its published text on 2026-07-13. Academic sources are summarised from their published claims and were not re-run.

Each source is classified as one of: **exact duplication**, **partial overlap**, **contextual background**, or **contribution**.

## Summary

No source publishes the project's central results. The overlap is still substantial. In one case, the Tamil Nadu Economic Survey 2025-26, an official document already publishes a Tamil Nadu manufacturing size-class distribution for the same 2023-24 year, and it already cites the missing-middle literature. Novelty claims are therefore stated narrowly. The project's contribution is:

1. the survey-separated, full-size-range, peer-state diagnostic, with disclosure and uncertainty reporting;
2. per-person outcome ratios at state × industry cells, which MoSPI does not compute;
3. Kitagawa-style composition-adjusted peer comparisons, which are not published elsewhere;
4. manufacturing-specific state worker earnings and job quality with design-based intervals, which the PLFS report does not publish; and
5. the finding-to-instrument policy map (see POLICY_EVIDENCE.md).

Overlapping official tables are cited wherever the project's exhibits sit near them.

## Source classifications

### ASUSE 2023-24 official report (MoSPI): partial overlap

Already published at state level, by broad activity including manufacturing: establishment counts (Table 5), workers (Tables 29-32), **GVA per worker (Table 36)**, GVA per establishment (Table 38), and **emoluments per hired worker (Table 34)**. The project's ASUSE overall state values therefore duplicate official statistics. They serve as reconciliation checks. They are not findings.

Not published: state-level size-band distributions (Table 42 is all-India only), state × NIC2 outcome detail, industry-standardized comparisons, and any missing-middle analysis. **The state-level ASUSE manufacturing size distribution is not published anywhere.**

### PLFS 2023-24 annual report (MoSPI): partial overlap

Already published at state level: regular earnings (Table 38), casual earnings (Table 39), and self-employment earnings (Table 40), all for all industries only. Job quality (Table 36) covers the non-agriculture universe only. The enterprise-size distribution (Table 37) is all-India only, and industry-level casual earnings (Table 51) are all-India only. RSEs are published only for LFPR, WPR, UR and industry distributions. No RSE is published for earnings or job-quality tables.

**Manufacturing-specific state earnings and job quality, with design-based standard errors and intervals, are not published.** The worker-side earnings and job-quality estimates in this project are its contribution.

### ASI Summary Results and Volume I (MoSPI; checked against the 2022-23 release, same architecture): partial overlap

Already published at state level: the distribution of factories in operation by employment size (Statements 13A and 13B; 14A and 14B for manufacturing only), using official bands from 0-14 up to 5000+. State × NIC2 GVA and emoluments are published as absolute totals (Volume I Tables 2a and 4a). Ratios are published only all-India by industry (Table 7, Statement 6) or all-industry by state (Statements 9A and 9B). **Ratios at state × industry level are never published.** No composition-standardized comparison exists. Statement 18 is a concentration table.

Caveats: the full ASI 2023-24 volumes could not be confirmed online (the site could not be scraped, and results were press-released on 2025-08-27). Whether state-level employment, as opposed to factory counts, by size class is published was not confirmed.

Project exhibits that show ASI factory-count size distributions cite Statements 13A and 14A. The per-person ratios at state × NIC2 level and the standardization are the project's contribution.

### Tamil Nadu DES ASI report 2022-23 (pooled): partial overlap

The Tamil Nadu Directorate of Economics and Statistics publishes "Report on ASI Tamilnadu 2022-23 (Pooled)". Its Table 4.10, "Principal characteristics of Factories in Operation by size of workers," is a Tamil Nadu factory size-class table on a finer pooled sample. The report also has district statements and Tamil Nadu × NIC totals without ratios. The vintage (2022-23) and the pooled frame differ. The table is cited beside any Tamil Nadu factory size-class exhibit.

### Tamil Nadu Economic Survey 2025-26 (State Planning Commission): closest existing analysis, partial overlap

Published around February 2026 at spc.tn.gov.in. It supersedes the 2024-25 edition for this check.

- **Table 4.7 (p.97):** "Distribution of Manufacturing units, Employment, and GVA by size class for Tamil Nadu and All India, 2023-24." This table **combines ASI and ASUSE into one distribution**. It uses the Mehrotra and Giri (2019) size definitions (micro under 10, small 10-19, medium 20-99), cited in footnote 13 (p.96). The missing-middle literature is therefore already invoked in an official Tamil Nadu document, though only to define size bands.
- **Table 4.1 (p.87):** state-wise rankings of factories, employment and GVA, 2023-24.
- **Table 4.11 (p.101):** share of employment in 100+ factories over time, Tamil Nadu against Gujarat, Maharashtra and India, with an explicit "dual pattern" narrative (para 4.9.2).
- **Section 4.3 (pp.90-91):** Tamil Nadu's factory wage share of GVA is about 15%, against 8% for Gujarat and 9% for Maharashtra. The emoluments share is 30%, against 18% and 24%. This anticipates the direction of the project's labour-share finding. The direction is corroborated. It is not new. The project's additions are the 2023-24 per-paid-person levels across all six comparators and the composition-adjusted decomposition.

Not in the Economic Survey: a peer-state size-band comparison across the full 1 to 250+ range; survey-separated frames (Table 4.7 merges the two surveys, which this project does not do); suppression and uncertainty reporting; adjusted decompositions; or worker-side job quality by state.

### Academic literature: contextual background

- Hsieh and Olken (2014), "The Missing 'Missing Middle'": firm-size distributions in India, Indonesia and Mexico decline continuously, with no discontinuity. This motivates the diagnostic. It contains no Tamil Nadu or 2023-24 analysis.
- Hsieh and Klenow (2009; 2014): misallocation dispersion; Indian plants grow less with age. Background for interpretation only.
- Mehrotra and Giri (2019), "The size structure of India's enterprises: Not just the middle is missing": a thematic predecessor. Its size definitions now appear in official Tamil Nadu use (Economic Survey Table 4.7 footnote).
- NSS 73rd Round (2015-16): predecessor unincorporated-sector survey, different vintage.
- 2023 small-establishment (5-49 workers) paper and 2016 family-to-hired-labour paper: background.

### Udyam registration statistics: contextual background

Tamil Nadu's Udyam base is 98.16% micro (3,300,096 of 3,362,057 as of 2025-03-31). This describes a registration universe. It is useful as context only.

### Tamil Nadu policy documents: inputs to the policy section

The MSME Policy Note 2025-26, the Industrial Policy 2021 and the Budget Speech 2025-26 are summarised in POLICY_EVIDENCE.md. They state objectives, instruments and outputs. None performs the statistical analysis in this project.

## Citation and positioning

1. The project does not claim to be the first size-class or labour-share analysis of Tamil Nadu manufacturing. The closest official analysis is Chapter 4 of the Economic Survey 2025-26 (Tables 4.7 and 4.11, and §4.3). The project is a survey-separated, peer-benchmarked complement with uncertainty reporting.
2. ASI Statements 13A and 14A and Tamil Nadu DES Table 4.10 are cited beside any factory size-class exhibit. ASUSE Tables 34 and 36 are cited beside the ASUSE overall values, which serve as reconciliation checks.
3. The labour-share direction may be attributed to the Economic Survey. Its levels (15% and 30%) could not be reconciled with official ASI 2023-24 totals, whereas this project's figures reconcile closely (EXTERNAL_VALIDATION.md, §5). The Survey's levels are not cited as corroboration.
4. The results that are not published elsewhere are: the ASUSE state size distribution, state × NIC2 per-person ratios, adjusted decompositions, and manufacturing-specific worker outcomes with intervals.
5. The ASI 2023-24 Summary Results were located on microdata.gov.in (catalog 256). The project's per-return size distribution reconciles with Statement 14A almost exactly (100+ unit share 28.42% against 28.43%). The equal-allocation series differs by the multi-unit adjustment, which is quantified in EXTERNAL_VALIDATION.md, §4.
