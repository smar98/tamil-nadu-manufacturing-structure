# What this project does not show

This note sets out the questions a skeptical reader is likely to ask, with the answer for each.

## Does the project show a "missing middle"?

No. The three displayed averages come from populations defined partly by hiring and factory-coverage rules. They do not form a harmonized firm-size histogram, and the surveys do not follow the same firms. The project treats "missing middle" only as a hypothesis. Testing it requires common size bands, peer benchmarks and longitudinal data.

## Does the 11.1x productivity ratio measure a registration effect?

No. Sector mix, capital intensity, firm age, selection, prices and differences in the labour denominator can generate the gap. The 11.1x registered-to-OAE ratio (payload field `headline.productivity_ratio_registered_to_oae`, value 11.094) is descriptive. It is not the return to registration.

## Are own-account establishments policy failures?

No. Many may be viable livelihood activities with no desired factory growth path. The policy section separates livelihood support from investigation of growth-oriented firms.

## Does the sector bubble chart identify constrained industries?

No. The chart measures employer incidence, productivity and establishment mass. A low hired-worker share may reflect production technology, home-based work, demand or preferences. A barrier that government can remove is only one of these possibilities. The chart identifies questions. It does not identify intervention targets.

## Do registered-sector GVA levels prove technological capability?

No. GVA per person engaged is affected by capital, prices, market power and industry composition. The registered-sector strip is a composition warning. It does not measure capability.

## Does PLFS validate ASUSE or ASI counts?

No. PLFS describes people, while ASUSE and ASI describe establishments. PLFS supports only the broad observation that manufacturing contains both wage work and self-employment.

## Could public aggregates expose respondents?

The web payload contains no unit identifiers. ASI aggregate construction suppresses cells with fewer than 10 sampled factories, or where one factory exceeds 70% of absolute weighted GVA. The site uses only high-stability ASI sectors with at least 100 sampled factories.

## Residual risks

1. ASI persons engaged and ASUSE workers are only approximately comparable.
2. Survey periods overlap but do not align exactly.
3. Detailed ASUSE sector uncertainty is not shown as confidence intervals.
4. ASI and ASUSE do not form a complete, mutually exclusive partition under every coverage edge case.
5. Administrative records are needed to study transitions and policy exposure.

## Summary

The project is a descriptive structural diagnostic and a research agenda. It is not a causal evaluation, and it does not show that medium-sized firms are missing.
